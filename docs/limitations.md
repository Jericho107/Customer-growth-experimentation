# Limitations

- All experiment counts are synthetic.
- The current design assumes a simple two-arm experiment and a nominal 50/50 allocation.
- The z-test uses a standard large-sample approximation; small-sample or rare-event settings may require different methods.
- Sample-size planning uses a normal approximation and does not model attrition, clustering or repeated-measure effects.
- Segment review is a governance demonstration and does not implement multiplicity correction across many exploratory segments.
- Sequential testing, CUPED, Bayesian methods and experimentation-platform integration are outside scope.
- No causal claim beyond the randomized synthetic setup or realised commercial uplift is made.
